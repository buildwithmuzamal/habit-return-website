Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my subscription?

If you previously purchased Habit Return Premium, you can restore your purchase from the subscription or paywall screen using the Restore Purchases option.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account used to make the purchase.

I purchased Premium but it isn't unlocked. What should I do?

First, make sure you're using the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Then try restoring your purchase.

If Premium is still not unlocked, contact us using the email above and include any relevant details about the issue.

How does the 7-day free access work?

Habit Return includes 7 days of free access starting when you first set up the app. After the free access period ends, a subscription is required to continue using Premium features.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account. You can manage or cancel your subscription from:

Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions

Habit Return cannot cancel an Apple subscription on your behalf.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete your Habit Return data from within the app. Deleting your data removes the locally stored Habit Return data from your device.

Your subscription is managed separately through your Apple Account and is not canceled by deleting app data.

Does Habit Return collect my personal data?

Habit Return is designed with privacy in mind. Your habit data is stored locally on your device.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
