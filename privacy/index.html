Habit Return is a habit-tracking app designed to help you stay consistent with your goals, including on days when you do not complete your usual routine.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Habit Return stores and how it is handled.

Information Habit Return Stores

Habit Return stores information that you choose to enter into the app, including:

  • Goals and habit details
  • Daily check-ins and habit progress
  • Reasons for not completing a habit
  • Minimum actions and other goal settings
  • Reminder preferences
  • Other settings related to your use of the app

This information is stored locally on your device. Habit Return does not upload your habit data to its own servers.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Habit Return schedules notifications locally on your device. These notifications remind you about your goals and habits. Habit Return does not send your notification content or reminder schedule to a Habit Return server.

You can disable reminders at any time from the app's settings.

Analytics and Tracking

Habit Return uses TelemetryDeck to understand how people use the app and to improve the experience.

Habit Return sends anonymous app usage events such as when onboarding is completed, a goal is created, a check-in is completed, or the subscription screen is viewed.

Habit Return does not send the content of your goals, reasons, minimum actions, or personal notes to TelemetryDeck.

TelemetryDeck receives an anonymized identifier, the events sent by Habit Return, a rounded timestamp, and limited device and app information such as app version, operating system version, and device type. TelemetryDeck states that it does not store IP addresses.

Habit Return does not use analytics for advertising or to track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.

Third-Party Services

Habit Return uses TelemetryDeck for privacy-focused analytics. Habit Return does not send your habit content to TelemetryDeck.

Subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store and managed through your Apple account. Habit Return does not collect or store your full payment card or billing information.

Habit Return does not require an account, and your goals, check-ins, and habit content are stored locally on your device.

Your Data

Your goals, check-ins, habit progress, and related settings are stored locally on your device. Habit Return does not have access to the habit data stored on your device.

Because your data is stored locally, Habit Return cannot recover it if it is deleted from your device or lost for another reason.

Deleting Your Data

Habit Return provides an option to delete your data from within the app. When you delete a goal, the goal's associated history and check-ins are deleted as well. If you choose to delete all Habit Return data, your locally stored goals, check-ins, and related data will be removed. Deleted data cannot be recovered through Habit Return.

Subscriptions

Habit Return offers a paid subscription that provides access to premium features.

New installations may receive a 7-day free access period before the subscription screen is shown. The applicable subscription price and terms are shown in the app before purchase.

Subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store and managed through your Apple account. Habit Return does not collect or store your full payment card or billing information.

Apple may provide Habit Return with information about your subscription status so the app can determine whether premium features are available.

You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple account's subscription settings.

Children's Privacy

Habit Return is not specifically designed for children, and Habit Return does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Habit Return may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. When this policy is updated, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
