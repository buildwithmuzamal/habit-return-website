Habit Return is a habit-tracking app designed to help you stay consistent with your goals, including on days when you do not complete your usual routine.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Habit Return stores and how it is handled.

Information Habit Return Stores

Habit Return stores information that you choose to enter into the app, including:

  • Goals and habit details
  • Daily check-ins and habit progress
  • Reasons for not completing a habit
  • Minimum actions and other goal settings
  • Reminder preferences
  • Other settings related to your use of the app

This information is stored locally on your device. Habit Return does not upload your habit data to its own servers.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Habit Return schedules notifications locally on your device. These notifications remind you about your goals and habits. Habit Return does not send your notification content or reminder schedule to a Habit Return server.

You can disable reminders at any time from the app's settings.

Analytics and Tracking

Habit Return does not use analytics services or third-party tracking. It does not use your habit data to track your activity across other apps or websites, and it does not contain advertising.

Third-Party Services

Habit Return does not currently use third-party services to collect or process your habit data. You do not need to create an account to use Habit Return, and your habit data is not stored on a Habit Return cloud server.

Your Data

Your goals, check-ins, habit progress, and related settings are stored locally on your device. Habit Return does not have access to the habit data stored on your device.

Because your data is stored locally, Habit Return cannot recover it if it is deleted from your device or lost for another reason.

Deleting Your Data

Habit Return provides an option to delete your data from within the app. When you delete a goal, the goal's associated history and check-ins are deleted as well. If you choose to delete all Habit Return data, your locally stored goals, check-ins, and related data will be removed. Deleted data cannot be recovered through Habit Return.

Subscriptions

Habit Return may offer optional paid subscriptions that provide access to premium features. Subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store and are managed through your Apple account.

Habit Return does not collect or store your full payment card or billing information. Apple may provide Habit Return with information about your subscription status so the app can determine whether premium features are available.

If a free trial is offered, the trial duration and subscription price will be clearly shown before you start the subscription. After a free trial ends, the subscription automatically renews unless you cancel it through your Apple account before the trial or current subscription period ends. You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple account's subscription settings.

Children's Privacy

Habit Return is not specifically designed for children, and Habit Return does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Habit Return may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. When this policy is updated, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
